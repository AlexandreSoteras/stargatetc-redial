4. StargateTC
   ----------
   payload/resource/background/800_3_c_loading.tga (release zip only) is
   StargateTC 2.0's menu background tile with a "STARGATETC REDIAL x.y.z"
   stamp added. Artwork (c) the Stargate TC team. Nothing else from
   StargateTC is included: you need your own copy of the mod.

5. Waypoints and bot names
   -----------------------
   Part of the waypoints and of the bot names come from Freeman551's
   "Bots for StargateTC" 0.2 pack (2012):
   https://www.moddb.com/mods/stargatetc/downloads/bots-for-stargatetc
   The other waypoints were generated and fixed for this project.
