jk_botti chat file - StargateTC edition (English)

These are comment lines. Anything before the first section header is
ignored. Each line of a section is one chat message. Keep lines short:
the loader reads at most 79 characters per line, and a longer line is
split into two messages. A section holds up to 100 lines. Use plain
ASCII only (no accents, no curly quotes), because the typo "humanizer"
works on single bytes.

Sections shared by both teams:
  [bot_chat]     random chat, checked every 30 s (bot_chat_percent)
  [bot_taunt]    after a kill (bot_taunt_percent)
  [bot_whine]    after being killed by a player (bot_whine_percent)
  [bot_endgame]  at the end of the map (bot_endgame_percent)
Team sections have the same meaning. A bot that has joined a team mixes
its team's lines with the shared ones, in proportion to the line counts:
  [bot_chat_tauri]  [bot_taunt_tauri]  [bot_whine_tauri]  [bot_endgame_tauri]
  [bot_chat_goauld] [bot_taunt_goauld] [bot_whine_goauld] [bot_endgame_goauld]
A section starts with its name in square brackets, alone on its line.

Placeholders (can be used several times in a line):
  %n  chat and endgame: the name of the bot speaking
      taunt: the player the bot just killed
      whine: the player who killed the bot
  %r  a random connected player (never the bot itself)

Bots add typos (dropped or swapped letters), sometimes switch to
lowercase and may strip clan tags from names: see bot_chat_drop_percent,
bot_chat_swap_percent, bot_chat_lower_percent and bot_chat_tag_percent
in jk_botti.cfg. A line starting with '!' is sent exactly as written:
the '!' is removed and no typos or lowercase are applied.
